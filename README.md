# 🌍 NomadFlow

> **Smart Travel Planning & Logistics Platform for Digital Nomads & Long-Term Travelers**  
> *Developed for Hack Brasil 2026 — Track 07: Logistics & Automation*

---

## 📌 The Problem
Planning multi-month journeys forces long-term travelers and digital nomads to navigate over **15 fragmented platforms** (transportation, lodging, local dining, and freelance income) [2, 5]. This causes:
* **Severe time loss** searching across disconnected booking and review sites [2, 3].
* **Unpredictable costs** with zero visibility over total cross-border expenses [3-5].
* **Logistical friction** when trying to balance remote work or local gigs with dynamic travel itineraries [2-4, 8].

---

## 💡 The Solution: NomadFlow
**NomadFlow** unifies the entire long-term travel lifecycle into a single, AI-driven logistics ecosystem [3, 4, 6]:

* 🚍 **Multi-Modal Route Optimization:** Calculates efficient bus, flight, and train connections under a strict global budget cap (e.g., R$ 5,000 for 30 days) [2-4, 7].
* 🏨 **Accommodation as a Local Hub:** Uses chosen lodging as the center point to map food, culture, and attractions within customizable 1 km, 3 km, or 5 km radii [4, 8].
* 💼 **Income & Freelance Leads:** Integrates remote work and local job opportunities to ensure financial sustainability during extended trips [2-4, 8].
* ⚡ **Dynamic Budget Re-Balancing:** Automatically recalculates itinerary choices when dates, locations, or budget limits change [4, 7, 9].

---

## 🎯 Why NomadFlow Beats Traditional Giants
Unlike legacy tools focused on short weekend getaways (like Decolar or Booking), NomadFlow is built specifically for **multi-destination continuous travel**:
* **Holistic Itineraries:** Solves the *entire* sequence (Route ➔ Stay ➔ Local Life ➔ Income) in one automated workflow [3, 10].
* **Autonomous Intelligence:** Dynamically scales routes and costs across multiple countries based on financial limits rather than isolated ticket searches [10].
